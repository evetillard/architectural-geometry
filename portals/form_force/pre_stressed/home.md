## Definition

A surface structure is said here to be self-prestressed when its geometry admits a non-zero, self-equilibrated state of internal stress. The forces acting within the surface balance at every internal point (or node in a discrete network) so that no transverse load is required to maintain its shape. Prestress must nevertheless be introduced during construction and transferred to the boundary supports: self- does not mean that prestress appear spontaneously, but that the internal forces balance one another.
