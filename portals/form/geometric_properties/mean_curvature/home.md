## Definition and properties

The mean curvature $H$ at a point on a surface is the average of the two principal curvatures:

$$
H = \frac{k_1 + k_2}{2}
$$

It describes the overall tendency of the surface to bend at that point.

When the mean curvature is zero at every point, the surface is called a **minimal surface**. In this case, the two principal curvatures are equal in magnitude and opposite in sign: $k_1 = -k_2$
