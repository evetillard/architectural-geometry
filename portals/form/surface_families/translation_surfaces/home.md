## Definition and properties

A translation surface is generated by translating one curve along another while preserving its shape and orientation. The two generating curves play interchangeable roles. 
A translation surface may be represented parametrically as : 
$$
\mathbf{S}(u,v) = \boldsymbol{g}(u) + \boldsymbol{h}(v)
$$
